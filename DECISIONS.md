# Decision log — ledger-sync

How the corpus becomes the three reports, and why each judgement call went the way it did.

## Result against the checkpoint

- **256** transactions produced; the checkpoint expects **257**.
- The one-transaction gap is **deliberate and explained**: 4821's bank balance drops ₹7,500 on 2026-07-29 (between 11:53 and 17:06) with **no SMS or email** evidencing it. It cannot be a ledger entry — the contract requires at least one `source_message_id`, and there is none — so it is reported in `reconciliation.json` instead. **256 accounted-for + 1 unaccountable = 257.**
- Per-account summary matches the checkpoint to the paise **except 4821 `spend`**, which is short by exactly that ₹7,500. 9075 reconciles exactly. Income, micro, and transfer totals match on both accounts.

I did not force the numbers to 257. Per the README, a number that doesn't match but explains itself is the honest outcome.

## Pipeline decisions

1. **Amount parsing (INC-2026-09-11).** The amount regex required two decimals, so whole-rupee amounts matched the `Avl Bal` instead. Made paise optional. See `incident/INC-2026-09-11-resolution.md`.

2. **`occurred_at` is the transaction time, never the arrival time.** SMS carry it inline; emails carry it in the `Date:` header. Using `received_at` would misplace transactions in time and break dedup (a re-uploaded SMS arrives weeks later but is the same transaction).

3. **Dedup key = account + direction + amount + `occurred_at` as an *instant*.** One transaction is evidenced by several messages (SMS + email + re-uploads). Keying on the instant (`toInstant()`), not the `OffsetDateTime`, matters: one email's `Date:` header is in UTC (`+0000`) while its SMS is IST (`+05:30`) — the same instant, different offset. An offset-sensitive key silently double-counts it. This double-count was masking the missing ₹7,500 and making the count *look* like a perfect 257.

4. **MICRO = a UPI debit of ₹100 or less.** The UPI check is required, not just the amount: a ₹47.33 debit to `SWIGGY` is ordinary spend, not micro. UPI is detected from `UPI/` in the merchant, which survives on both SMS and email.

5. **TRANSFER = a debit on one account matched by a credit on the *other* account (same amount, within ~15 min) — decided from the money, not the words.** The merchant text lies: `NEFT INWARD SELF` says "SELF" but has no matching leg (money from an untracked account → INCOME), and `IMPS/P2A/RAHUL SHARMA` is a real payment to another person (→ SPEND). Only cross-account matched pairs are transfers. This yields exactly the checkpoint's 25000 / 6000.

6. **Reconciliation walks the stated-balance chain.** Every SMS quotes the balance after the transaction. In time order, the stated balance must move by exactly the transaction amount; where it moves by more, an unrecorded transaction happened between the two messages. `NormalizedTxn` (frozen) doesn't carry the balance, so reconciliation re-reads the corpus.

7. **A credit limit is not a balance.** The card (3310) quotes `Avl Limit`, which swings from bill payments and statement credits that never appear as card messages, so it cannot be reconciled transaction-by-transaction. Reconciliation skips `Avl Limit` messages. This is a general rule (limit ≠ balance), not a hardcoded account.

8. **`report` is scoped to corpus-a.** `V2__seed.sql` holds pre-corpus (June) legacy rows — already baked into the July opening balance, and the input to the separate Backfill exercise. They are excluded from `ledger.json`/`summary.json` (filtered by their `m-legacy-*` ids). Open question worth confirming with the team.

9. **H2 migration fix.** `V1__initial.sql` used `IDENTITY` as a column type, which H2 2.x (pinned in `build.gradle`) removed. Changed to `BIGINT GENERATED BY DEFAULT AS IDENTITY`. Rewriting a shipped migration is normally a smell; done here only because the pinned driver rejects the original.

## Framework choice

None — JDK 21 only, as the scaffolding intends (hand-written JSON, JDBC over H2). The problem is parsing and reconciliation logic, not plumbing; a framework would add dependency weight without buying anything. Money is `BigDecimal` throughout; times keep their offset and are compared as instants.
